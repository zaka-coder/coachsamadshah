My approach

Simple things, done every week

Most people don't fail because they don't know what to do. They fail because the plan doesn't fit their life, the diet ignores the food cooked at home, and nobody checks on them.

So my coaching is built on three things: training you can stick to, food you already eat, and a weekly check-in where we look at the numbers together and adjust.

Whether I'm working with Shaheen before a series or with someone walking into a gym for the first time, the principles are the same. Only the level changes.
