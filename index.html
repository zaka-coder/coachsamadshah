What are the gym fees in Peshawar at Shaheen Run 4 Life?

Gym membership is Rs 6,000 per month. Group classes, 1:1 coaching and the cricket programme are listed in the prices section above.

Is there a ladies gym / ladies timing?

Yes — we have separate ladies timings in a comfortable environment. Message us on WhatsApp for the current schedule.

Can I train with Coach Samad online?

Yes. Online coaching includes a custom training plan, a desi diet plan and weekly WhatsApp check-ins, anywhere in Pakistan or abroad.

I've never been to a gym. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Most of our clients started as complete beginners. Your plan starts at your level and builds up safely.

Do you train young cricketers?

Yes — our cricket fitness programme works with academy and club players, including young fast bowlers.
