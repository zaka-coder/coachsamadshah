Why 1:1 instead of a group?

Faster results. Fewer mistakes.

In a group class, the coach watches 30 people. In 1:1, every rep, every meal and every week is watched for you. That's why it costs more — and why it usually works faster.

Personal training FAQ

How much does a personal trainer cost in Peshawar?

1:1 personal training with Coach Samad is Rs 20,000 per month, including a custom training plan, a desi diet plan and weekly check-ins. See all fees on the prices page.

Personal trainer vs gym trainer — what's the difference?

Most gym trainers in Peshawar walk the floor and help many members at once. A personal trainer works one-to-one with you: a custom plan, every session coached, and weekly progress checks. That's what 1:1 coaching with Coach Samad is.

I've never trained before. Is 1:1 right for me?

Yes — beginners often get the most from 1:1, because you learn correct form and good habits from the start instead of copying people on social media.

What times are available?

Coach Samad is at the gym Monday to Saturday, 6 am to 11 pm. Your session times are agreed with you at the assessment.

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No. Your diet plan is built from normal Pakistani home food — roti, daal, chicken, rice — in the right amounts. No expensive "diet food" needed.

Can I train with Coach Samad if I'm not in Peshawar?

Yes, through online coaching anywhere in Pakistan or abroad.
