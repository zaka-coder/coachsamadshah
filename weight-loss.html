The 12 weeks

How the programme runs

Every client's numbers are different. This is the structure we follow and adjust for you.

Weeks 1–4

Build the base

Assessment, measurements and photos. Learn the exercises properly, fix eating habits, and build the routine of showing up.

Weeks 5–8

Push the pace

Training gets harder, the diet gets fine-tuned from your weekly results. This is where most of the visible change happens.

Weeks 9–12

Finish & keep it

Final push, final measurements and photos — and a plan to keep the weight off after the 12 weeks end.

Questions

Weight loss FAQ

How much weight can I lose in 12 weeks?

It depends on your starting weight, your consistency and your body. We'll give you a realistic target at your free assessment — we won't promise a number we can't stand behind.

Do I need to stop eating roti and rice?

No. Your plan uses normal home food in the right portions. Cutting out everything you enjoy is the main reason diets fail.

Do you give fat burners or weight loss pills?

No. This programme is training, food and habits — the things that actually last.

What's the difference between Rs 15,000 and Rs 20,000?

Rs 15,000/month covers the coached classes, diet plan and check-ins. Rs 20,000/month adds full gym access so you can also train on your own outside class.

Is there a weight loss programme for ladies?

Yes — the same programme runs in our separate ladies timings. See ladies training.
